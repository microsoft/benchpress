**Baselines on the 59/25 model split.** Probes are selected on 59 models; all 25 held-out models are evaluated at each budget. Panels (a,b) show median absolute error (MedAE); (c,d) show median absolute percentage error (MedAPE), both pooled over unrevealed scores (lower is better). The methods use identical revealed scores and test cells within each panel at each budget. Requested probes may be unavailable, and newly revealed scores leave the test set. Offsets only removes the two factors; model mean uses logit space.
